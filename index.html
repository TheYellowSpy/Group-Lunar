Welcome To The Official Group Lunar Website!

Who Are You?

We are a small minecraft group in the server Aeroworks. There are currently three people in our group which are RenPT23, The_Yellow_Spy and GalaxyspyMC.

What Is Your Goal?

Our goal is to make buying things easy, cheap and accessible. We currently can't fulfill all of what we want because of limitations of our size and resources but we hope that in the future that all of these things will be fulfilled.

What Have You made?

We currently have a shop called Lunar Shop but thats basicly it, because we dont have alot of resources we cant really make any thing more then that. In the future we hope to see more shops and also better shops.

How Do I Join?

To join lunar you must download the modpack associated with the Aeroworks server. You get the modpack by opening modrinth, going into discover, searching "Aeroworks" and download the one which was uploaded by "gguppy". Then you need to launch the game and join the server by pressing the big "join server" button and when you're in there please ask one of the members which i stated before if you could join Lunar.
